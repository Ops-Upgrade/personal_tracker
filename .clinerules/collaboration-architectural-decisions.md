## Brief overview
  Rules mandating collaborative decision-making with the human on architectural choices and design decisions. Applies globally across all workspaces.

## Human collaboration
  - Work collaboratively with the human on the code at all times.
  - For all architectural decisions, you must explicitly ask the human before proceeding.
  - If you question any choices or identify potential issues, inform the human with your findings, confirm the details, and discuss plans with appropriate questions before finalizing them.
