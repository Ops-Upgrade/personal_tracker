## Brief overview
  Rules ensuring thorough, researched, and diligent work — never lazy or surface-level. Applies globally across all workspaces.

## Thoroughness and diligence
  - NEVER take shortcuts or produce lazy, surface-level work. Every response must reflect a thorough, researched, and deliberate effort.
  - Before writing code or proposing a solution, exhaust all available tools — search the web, read documentation, inspect the codebase, and review prior context — to build a concrete understanding of the problem space.
  - When multiple approaches exist, research and evaluate them before selecting one. Do not default to the first pattern that comes to mind.
